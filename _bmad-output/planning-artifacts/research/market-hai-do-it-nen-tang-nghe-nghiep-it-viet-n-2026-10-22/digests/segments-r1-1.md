## (b) Leads worth chasing (contradictions first)

1. **Japan-partner firm counts disagree.** VCCI says more than 300 VN firms serve Japan; a VietnamNews snippet says more than 400 collaborate with Japan. The 6,000-engineers-in-Japan figure has no stated method. Check JISA/JETRO, Japan's immigration statistics (the "Engineer/Specialist in Humanities" visa by nationality), or VINASA.
2. **Junior hiring is described two ways.** ITviec says 60% of firms still plan to expand (a contraction framed as normal). JobOKO says fresher demand is −13% and IT postings −17%. Meanwhile ITviec reports total postings +12% in Q1/2025. The time windows and data sources differ, so reconcile them before claiming an "oversupply".
3. **ITviec full PDFs.** https://itviec.com/salary-reports/download/fbd9e3ed-f64f-431f-94c1-c10783a519fc?locale=vi is over 10MB, so download and parse it locally. The 2023-24 PDF is mirrored at https://file.unica.vn/storage/3ed0f159b311e7c68bb73578666d93bb3185bb24/itviec-it-salary-report-2023-2024-full-vi-bc54d618.pdf. These likely contain job-choice factors, job-change intent, channels and AI usage, which are the core numbers for Q1, Q2 and Q5.
4. **TopDev report** (form-gated, https://topdev.vn/page/bao-cao-it-viet-nam): developer-persona and learning-channel data.
5. **ITviec AI & IT Recruitment mini-report** (n=846, Jun–Jul 2025): 73% of firms use AI widely, but only 5.4% fully trust its output. URL: https://itviec.com/blog/tom-tat-chinh-bao-cao-ung-dung-ai-va-tuyen-dung-it-tai-vietnam/ (not fetched).
6. **TopCV paid candidate features.** https://www.topcv.vn/dieu-kien-giao-dich-chung returned 403. The search snippet confirms TopCV sells a "Nâng cấp tài khoản" (premium candidate account) tier, but no prices were found. Try the TopCV app store listing or the upgrade page directly.
7. Navigos/VietnamWorks Talent Guide 2026, and the VietnamWorks figure that 60% of laid-off IT staff were still jobless after one year: find the original release.

## (c) Looked for, could not find (this run)

- **IT-specific job-search channel shares** (ITviec, TopDev, LinkedIn, Facebook groups, referrals, headhunters). Nothing current or IT-specific; the only figure is the general-workforce Viet Research 2023 result (48.8% social).
- **Ranked job-choice factors with percentages** (salary, career path, remote work). These exist in the ITviec and TopDev reports but are gated.
- **Willingness to pay:** no VN evidence on candidates paying for CV services, TopCV Pro prices, LinkedIn Premium adoption in VN, or local coaching or mock-interview prices in VND. **Q4 is effectively unanswered.**
- **Vietnam-specific AI/ChatGPT use for CVs or interview prep:** none found. Only the global Canva figure (45%, n=5,000).
- **Bootcamp and online-course usage and prices** (MindX, CodeGym, FUNiX, Coursera in VN): not searched, out of budget.
- **Size of the remote-for-foreign-companies segment:** not found.
- **Two-source rule:** no quantitative claim in this digest has an independently fetched second source. Every number is single-source.
