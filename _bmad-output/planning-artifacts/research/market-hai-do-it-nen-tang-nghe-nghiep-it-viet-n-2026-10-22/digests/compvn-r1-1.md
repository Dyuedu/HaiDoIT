## (c) Leads (contradictions first)

1. **TopCV user count contradicts itself** on the same page (10M+ in EN vs 9.5M+ in VN), and earlier press gave 4M or 7M users and 90k or 200k clients. Revenue is still unverified. Next queries: "TopCV báo cáo tài chính", "Công ty Cổ phần TopCV Việt Nam doanh thu", "TopCV IPO".
2. **Crowdsourced reviews carry regulatory risk.** MIC blocked reviewcongty in 2023 and business groups lobby against anonymous review sites (14). That bears directly on the "employer response index" feature. Check Decree 147/2024 and its rules on social-network operators.
3. **The salary-data gap is real.** ITviec, the strongest IT salary source, publishes static medians behind a lead-gen form (8). Nobody I found offers live percentiles. Check whether ITviec has an on-site "salary check" that my fetch missed.
4. **Gross-net is commoditized and free** (TopCV, TopDev, TVPL, several apps). An iOS app ("Tính Lương", 16) already pitches offer comparison and contract checks, so treat this feature as table stakes, not a wedge.
5. **Incumbents' AI goes to employers.** VietnamWorks' Job Post AI (Apr 2026) and ITviec's AI Match point in that direction. A candidate-side AI advisor looks unoccupied among incumbents, but fast-follow risk is high because they own the job data.
6. TopCV's Japanese investor Mynavi could make TopCV a natural entrant on the Japan/BrSE path. This is an unverified belief.
