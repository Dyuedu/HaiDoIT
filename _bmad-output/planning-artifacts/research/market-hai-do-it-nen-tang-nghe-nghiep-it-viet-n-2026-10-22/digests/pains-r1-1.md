## (b) Pain-severity ranking of the candidate features (based on evidence from this run)

- **Strong**
  - **Junior/AI direction pain**, which feeds the 90-day roadmap, AI-automation index, skill half-life and what-if simulation. This rests on row 8, persists 2023→2026, and has named voices. Note that the evidence shows the *pain*, not willingness to pay for an AI advisor.
  - **Offer comparison with social-insurance base check.** 43% / 0.12% from Navigos (row 1). The prevalence is large, but it comes from a single source.
- **Moderate**
  - **Employer ghosting index.** 58% (vendor survey), the Voz voice, and international data showing a rising trend.
  - **Ghost/zombie posting detection.** International data is strong. There is no Vietnam-specific ghost-job number.
  - **Scam detection.** The pattern persists, and one case ran through TopCV, but job scams are not a top-5 fraud type in 2025, overall fraud is declining, incumbents already moderate posts, and victims are mostly non-IT.
- **Weak**
  - **Labor-contract clause checker.** Legal ambiguity is real, but I found no complaint volume from IT workers. I found nothing on the 13th-month bonus and resignation-timing angle.
- **No evidence retrieved (not tested this run)**
  - City demand map
  - Salary percentiles (opacity in general, beyond social insurance)
  - Anonymized career paths
  - Skill passport
  - Remote vs Japan / BrSE. The only hint: TopCV lists "bridge software engineer" as the 2nd hardest role to fill (9.92%, row 8), which suggests demand for BrSE.

## (c) Leads (contradictions first)

1. **Contradiction on IT demand:** JobOKO reports IT postings -16.9% YoY (2025), while ITviec reports +12% (Q1 2025) and 66.1% of firms expanding. Fetch the JobOKO and ITviec reports directly.
2. **Contradiction on fraud scale:** about 6,000 billion VND (Bộ Công an, 11 months of 2025, via LSVN) vs about 8,000 billion VND and 4,200 cases (Báo Lào Cai sidebar). Also, NCA shows victims falling. Look for the Bộ Công an or Cục A05 year-end 2025 report and any breakdown for job scams.
3. Navigos' 43% social-insurance figure: get the original report PDF and look for an IT-sector cut. Find a second source, such as VSS (BHXH VN) inspection results on "đóng không đúng mức lương".
4. CareerLink's 58% ghosting figure needs its methodology. TopCV and ITviec may have their own candidate-experience surveys.
5. Voz thread p/42201120 is blocked to the fetcher (403). Try a cached copy or Reddit r/vozforums threads.
