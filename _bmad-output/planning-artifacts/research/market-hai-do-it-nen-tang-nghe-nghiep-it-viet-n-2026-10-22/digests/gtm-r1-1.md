## (b) Regulatory risk per planned data practice

| Practice | Main rules | Severity | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Crawling job boards** | PDPL (recruiter names/contacts in posts are personal data); IP Law/database copyright (no VN-specific source found); competitor ToS (contract) | **Medium-High** | No VN source on scraping legality was found. A Taiwanese case found database-scraping to be copyright infringement (Lee and Li, off-jurisdiction). Re-selling scraped data that contains personal data risks the "trading personal data" fine (10x proceeds / ≥ VND 3bn). Mitigation: strip personal fields and store aggregates or facts only. |
| **CV / GitHub storage** | PDPL Art. 25, explicit consent, deletion duty, Decree 356 | **High** if CVs are shared with employers (that makes the platform a recruitment processor). Medium if used only for self-advice. | Consent must be explicit and demonstrable. CVs may contain sensitive data (health, location). Using foreign LLM APIs on CVs is a **cross-border transfer**: an impact assessment must be filed with MPS within 60 days, with fines up to 5% of revenue. |
| **Salary crowdsourcing** | PDPL (salary is personal data; possibly "financial" sensitive data, which is contested) | **Medium** | Low if anonymised at collection (aggregation, k-anonymity, no employer+title+date combos that identify a person). Classification is unresolved (finding 13). |
| **Anonymised career histories** | PDPL (re-identification risk) | **Medium** | Small IT companies make re-identification easy. Anonymisation standards were not found. |
| **Labor-contract upload** | PDPL (contracts contain ID numbers, salary, address, possibly bank data) | **High** | Densest personal data in the product. Third-party (employer) data is included. Needs strict consent, retention limits, and an in-VN storage choice. |
| **AI legal/tax advice agent** | Law on Lawyers and tax-agent rules (not retrieved, unverified); AI Law 134/2025 transparency and high-risk duties | **High (unverified)** | No VN source was found on AI legal advice. The Japan analogue (MOJ guidelines, 2026-08): paid, case-specific contract analysis/redrafting can be unauthorised practice; research/drafting aids are OK with safeguards. Unverified belief: in VN, paid "legal consulting" is reserved to lawyers or legal-consulting organisations. Position the feature as "information/explanation" plus referral to a lawyer. |
| **Job matching / introducing candidates to employers** | Law 74/2025 Art. 28; Decree 352/2025 | **High (business-model gating)** | If the product does "giới thiệu việc làm"/matching for fees, it likely needs an employment-service licence (VND 300M deposit) plus e-commerce compliance. Pure market-intelligence or career content may fall outside, but the activity list was not read in full. |

## (c) Leads (contradictions first)

1. **ChatGPT VN billing:** phongvu (2025-10) says VND local billing; fptshop (~2026-07) says USD only, with no MoMo/ZaloPay; gptprompts.ai (2026-05) says Go is about 211k VND. Check openai.com/chatgpt/pricing from a VN IP. This matters as the B2C anchor (about 130k VND/month is the cheapest global AI plan).
2. **Salary data as "sensitive"**: Le & Tran says not sensitive; other summaries say "financial information" is sensitive. Read Decree 356's sensitive-data list on vbpl.vn.
3. **Incumbent licensing**: ITviec's Quy chế shows no employment-service licence or MOIT registration. Check whether ITviec, TopCV and VietnamWorks hold licences (the DOLISA/MOHA registry). If they don't, enforcement is lax; if they do, it is the market norm.
4. Read the Law 74/2025 article that lists "dịch vụ việc làm" activities (vbpl.vn or chinhphu.vn) to see whether career counselling or labor-market information alone requires a licence.
5. The AI Law high-risk list (PM decision) and labelling decree, to see whether HR/recruitment AI is listed. Check MOST (Bộ KH&CN) drafts.
6. TopCV paid candidate features (e.g., "TopCV Pro"/CV templates) would be the only likely B2C revenue evidence. The page returned 403, so try the app store listing for in-app purchases.
7. VietnamWorks Job Post AI (2026-04): incumbents are already shipping AI on the employer side.
