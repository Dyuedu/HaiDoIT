Interpretation (inference, not evidence): TopCV, the market leader, prices candidate upgrades at 10k–500k VND, which is about 0.4–5% of a junior's 10–14M monthly salary. Its entry tier is only 50k VND. That points to a micro-transaction ceiling for B2C, with meaningful revenue more likely from employers (TopCV's core) or from learning products (ELSA-style subscriptions at 145k/month, plus deferred-pay bootcamps). This rests on single-source price points, so verify before relying on it.
