## (b) Lessons table

| Analogue | Maps to planned feature | What worked / failed |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn Premium ($2B/yr) | AI advisor, job-seeker premium | Candidates do pay at huge scale, but only on top of a network effect and an existing identity graph. The subscription growth sits beside a soft Talent Solutions business. |
| Levels.fyi | Salary percentiles, offer comparison, negotiation | Crowdsourced salary data was the traffic engine. The money came from a high-ticket human negotiation service ($249) and, per unverified belief, employer-side products. This is the closest validated path for "offer comparison + negotiation". |
| Final Round AI, Teal | AI negotiation and interview practice, 90-day roadmap | AI candidate tools raise seed or Series A rounds ($7M, $19M) at about $25–29/month or weekly pricing. That price point is mismatched with Vietnamese purchasing power (inference). Neither discloses ARR. Final Round's "refund if hired" offer suggests churn and retention pressure (inference). |
| Triplebyte | Verified skill passport | Failed. Candidate-side verification only made money via employer fees. It scaled volume poorly and broke trust over a privacy issue. Only the assessment technology survived, sold to Karat (a B2B interviewing firm). |
| Hired | Marketplace / employer response | About $130M raised, then sold under creditor pressure and absorbed into LHH. Two-sided tech marketplaces are fragile in hiring downturns. |
| Findy (JP) | Skill passport from GitHub, salary prediction | Worked. A GitHub-derived score works as a free hook for candidates, monetized B2B via scouting and job matching. It reached about 200–290K members and a ¥2.05B Series D. This is the strongest analogue. |
| LAPRAS (JP) | Skill passport (crawled profiles) | Slower: about 40K users after about 7 years and about ¥1.2B raised. Same B2B scouting model, smaller scale. Opt-in and privacy matter. |
| Glassdoor / Indeed | Employer response index, reviews | The employer-review business got folded into the parent amid repeated layoffs. Reviews are a feature of a jobs marketplace, not a standalone business. |
| Google Career Dreamer | Career path map, AI advisor | A free big-tech substitute using Lightcast data, which commoditizes "career path exploration". It is US-only for now. |
| Glints (SEA) | Whole platform, VN market | VC-funded SEA career platforms hit the 2022 downturn and cut staff. Its revenue model is employer-side recruitment. |
| Ghost-job extensions | Job-posting scanner | No monetization evidence (indie, $0 MRR). Best treated as a free trust and acquisition feature, not a paid product. |

Answer to Q3 (market intelligence plus AI coaching combined): no single global player found combines labor-market intelligence with a multi-agent advisor. The closest are Career Dreamer (Lightcast data plus Gemini, free) and Findy (score plus salary prediction plus B2B matching). This gap is a fit gap, not proof of demand.

## (c) Leads (contradictions first)

1. Levels.fyi funding: "no outside funding" (Latka) vs a VC deal (PitchBook, 2021) and a seed (CB Insights, 2022). Its current revenue mix, including employer and B2B data products, is unverified.
2. Teal+ pricing: three conflicting weekly prices ($9 and $13). Check the vendor page with a browser.
3. Findy members: about 200K (May 2025), about 267K (Sep 2026 job ad), about 290K (PR). The timing of each figure is unclear.
4. Hired: the brief assumed a "2025 shutdown", but the evidence shows absorption into LHH in Jun 2024. There is an anecdotal report of LHH layoffs in 2026.
5. Not yet checked: Findy's B2B products (Findy Team+ dev productivity), which may be a bigger revenue driver than the job-matching service. Check Findy's PR/IR.
6. Not yet checked: Final Round AI's actual ARR (Sacra or the press). Its 10M-user claim needs independent verification.
