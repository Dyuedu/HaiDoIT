### How the gaps resolve
- **Gap 1 (developer count): not resolved with two independent sources.** The figures measure different populations. About 480k (TopDev 2022) to about 560k (TopDev 2024) is the employed software and IT workforce; this is one publisher. More than 1.5M (GitHub 2023, accounts) and 1.5M (VINASA) measure broader populations. 1.2M (Bộ KH&CN) is the whole ICT workforce. Use **about 500–560k employed developers** as the working number for the addressable market and label it single-source (TopDev, consistent with VCCI's ~500k from round 1, which may itself derive from TopDev).
- **Gap 2 (graduates): partly reconciled.** TopDev gives 55–60k *enrolments* a year (2024) and 57k *graduates* (2022). The ~50k (Nhân Dân) and ~70k (VCCI) figures probably differ in scope: universities only, versus including colleges and vocational schools. I found no official figure from MOET or GSO. Working range: **50–60k graduates a year from universities**.
- **Gap 3 (demand direction): mixed.** ITviec posts +12% for Q1 and +7% for Q2 (platform only). JobOKO's whole market is +2.05%, and its IT figure is unconfirmed. TopCV 2024-25 is about −1% for IT, and its 2025-26 ranking is unconfirmed.
- **Gap 4 (job switching): resolved with two sources.** ITviec: 40.9% plan to quit within 12 months. JobsGO: 54.5% are actively searching.
- **Gap 5 (ITviec PDFs): extracted** (rows 4–6 and 9–13). The 2025-26 PDF and its "trì hoãn chuyển việc" (delaying job changes) data were not obtained; the fbd9e3ed link is the 2024-25 edition.
- **Gap 6 (online recruitment revenue): not found.**
