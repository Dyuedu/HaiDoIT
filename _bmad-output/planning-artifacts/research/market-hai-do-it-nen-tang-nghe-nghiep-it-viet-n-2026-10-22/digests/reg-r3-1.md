## (b) Answer to the decision

**Can the startup launch without an employment-service licence?** It can for a narrow MVP: published content, educational career roadmaps and generic AI career Q&A, with no matching of named jobs or candidates and no fees tied to placement. A full licence is needed before any job recommendation, job listing, candidate introduction, or monetised labour-market data or reports. Confidence: medium.

The reasoning:
- Art. 27(1) lists "tư vấn … việc làm" and the "thu thập, phân tích, lưu trữ, cung cấp thông tin về thị trường lao động" activity as employment services (F1).
- The licence obligation falls on "doanh nghiệp hoạt động dịch vụ việc làm", meaning an enterprise registered under enterprise law (F2, F3). The fine applies to "hoạt động dịch vụ việc làm" without a licence, whether or not fees are charged (F7).
- No retrieved text exempts free or information-only services. No retrieved text limits the licence to fee-charging activity.
- So a registered company that systematically publishes IT labour-market statistics and gives career counselling is literally inside the definition, even for free.
- In practice, licensed peers such as ITviec (F5) hold the licence and show it, and Decree 352 links licensed operators' websites to the National Employment Exchange (F4).
- Low-risk path: present the content as editorial or educational material (salary reports, roadmaps), not as personalised "tư vấn việc làm". Leave out job recommendations and job listings until the company holds a licence: VND 300m deposit, premises, qualified legal representative, and provincial approval.
- The second-source check is partial. The Q1 conclusion rests on F1/F2 plus F3/F7. The Q3 practice point (peers hold a licence) rests on ITviec alone. VietnamWorks' footer does not show one.

On legal information (Q5): Decree 77/2008 still applies in part (F8). This run found nothing on whether a non-lawyer company may give free general legal information. The safe assumption is to frame any such output as general information, not "tư vấn pháp luật".
