## (b) Per-practice risk table

| Practice | Legal basis | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job listings plus matching/recommendation (free or paid, online) | Law 74/2025 Art. 27(1), (4); Art. 28 licence and deposit; Decree 352/2025 | HIGH: online job introduction or matching is an "employment service" that needs a licence (F1–F3) | Launch without matching; link out to the original job board; or obtain the licence (deposit and staff) or partner with a licensed operator. Get a lawyer's opinion on whether pure aggregation is "giới thiệu việc làm". |
| Labour-market information / salary insights | Art. 27(1) lists "thu thập, phân tích, lưu trữ, cung cấp thông tin thị trường lao động" as an employment service; Art. 20 includes wages | MEDIUM-HIGH: the text literally covers it. Whether informational publishing alone triggers the licence is UNRESOLVED (Decree 352 not read). | Present as editorial/statistical content; check Decree 352 scope; seek a written opinion from MoHA/DoHA. |
| Career counselling ("tư vấn nghề") | Art. 27(1) "tư vấn ... việc làm" | MEDIUM: job counselling is listed. Pure skills or learning advice is probably outside it (unverified). | Frame as skills/education guidance, not job placement. |
| Crowdsourced salaries / anonymous employer ratings | Decree 147/2024 (verified accounts, 24h takedown); 2023 MIC block of reviewcongty (F10) | HIGH for anonymous company reviews: precedent of a block, and anonymous posting conflicts with phone verification. | Register or notify as a social network if needed; require VN-phone verification (pseudonymous display is fine); moderation plus a 24h takedown SLA; aggregate salary data only (k-anonymity thresholds); avoid named-person allegations. |
| CVs, GitHub analysis, contract uploads | PDPL 91/2025 + Decree 356/2025 Art. 4 | MEDIUM: salary and employment history are not listed as sensitive (F6), but contracts can contain ID-card images and bank details, which are sensitive, and behaviour tracking is sensitive. | Explicit consent; redact ID and bank fields on upload; data minimisation; deletion; do not process ID images. |
| Foreign LLM APIs (cross-border transfer) | PDPL Art. 22 (cross-border impact-assessment dossier); Art. 38 relief | MEDIUM: startup relief from Art. 21/22 for 5 years from 01/01/2026, LOST if processing sensitive data or a "large number" of subjects (threshold not found). | Strip or pseudonymise before API calls; prepare the transfer impact-assessment dossier anyway; DPA with the vendor. |
| Paid "AI legal agent" reviewing contracts | Law on Lawyers Art. 32 (practice only via law offices/firms) | HIGH if marketed as legal advice for a fee; no AI-specific rule found | Frame as a "legal information / checklist" tool with a disclaimer; partner with a licensed law firm for real advice; avoid "tư vấn pháp luật" wording. |
| AI tax / net-salary calculator | Tax Administration Law 2025 (01/7/2026) tax-agent services include "tư vấn thuế" | LOW for a deterministic calculator; MEDIUM if it gives personalised tax advice or files on users' behalf | Calculator plus disclaimer; no filing on behalf of users; partner with a tax agent. |
| AI negotiation practice | AI Law 134/2025; Decision 33/2026 (not high-risk) | LOW | Transparency/AI labelling (details not verified). |
| HR/recruitment AI matching | Decision 33/2026 does not list employment; general Art. 9 test still applies (F8) | LOW-MEDIUM | Keep human-in-the-loop; document the risk self-classification. |
| Crawling job boards | Copyright on compilations (Law on IP); site ToS; PDPL if postings contain recruiter personal data | MEDIUM (no case law found) | Prefer partnerships/feeds; store facts only, not copied descriptions; respect robots.txt and ToS; link back to the source. |

**Safest first-launch features:** net-salary/tax calculator (deterministic), negotiation practice, CV/GitHub skill analysis (with consent and minimisation), and aggregated salary statistics with no personal attribution. **Defer** until licensed or advised: job matching and listings, anonymous employer reviews, paid legal-contract review.

## (c) Leads
- Read Decree 352/2025/NĐ-CP (vbpl .doc link above) for the deposit amount, and for whether "cung cấp thông tin thị trường lao động" by private firms needs a licence.
- Find how TopCV and ITviec are licensed (check the MoHA/DoHA public list of licensed employment-service enterprises, and the e-commerce registration footer on online.gov.vn).
- Decree 147/2024 article numbers: social-network licence vs notification thresholds; Art. 23-ish verification.
- Decree 142/2026/NĐ-CP (AI Law guidance): transparency and labelling for generative AI; effective date of AI Law 134/2025 (15/8/2026 implied by the VOV transition date, unverified).
- PDPL threshold for "large number of data subjects" (Decree 356); Art. 33(2) content; penalty levels.
- Decree 77/2008 on legal consultancy (tư vấn pháp luật) / whether a newer decree replaced it.
- ITviec/TopCV ToS anti-scraping clauses (not fetched).
