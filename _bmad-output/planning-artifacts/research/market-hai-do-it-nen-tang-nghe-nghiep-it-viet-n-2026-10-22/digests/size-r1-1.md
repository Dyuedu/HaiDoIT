## (b) Leads worth chasing (contradictions first)
- **Workforce denominator contradiction:** 1.2M (MOST) vs 1.5M (VINASA) vs about 560k (TopDev, software only) vs about 400k (2023). The reachable market (software juniors/mids) is probably about 0.4–0.6M plus about 50k new graduates/yr, not 1.2M+. Get the TopDev 2025 PDF (gated at topdev.vn/get-report-en) or MOST's white book (Sách trắng CNTT-TT 2025).
- **Shortage vs junior oversupply:** the official narrative says shortage (150–700k), while market data (ITviec AI-suppression, TopCV "dư người code cơ bản", 2023 layoffs) says juniors are oversupplied. This tension is the core opportunity signal for career guidance. Verify it with junior-specific numbers (applications per junior posting, fresher time-to-hire).
- **TopCV IT demand rank, #2 vs #3 (36.46%):** fetch the original TopCV "Báo cáo thị trường tuyển dụng 2025–2026" PDF.
- **JobsGO ">50% IT workers want to change jobs":** fetch the Tuổi Trẻ URL https://tuoitre.vn/plo/xu-huong-tim-viec-nganh-it-nam-2025-rao-can-vo-hinh-giua-ung-vien-va-doanh-nghiep-post834396.html.
- **TopCV/Navigos/ITviec revenue:** check financial statements on masothue / Vietstock / dangkykinhdoanh, or DealStreetAsia (https://dealstreetasia.com/?p=360017). Navigos is owned by en-japan, so en-japan IR filings may break out Vietnam revenue.
- **ITviec full PDF** (https://itviec.com/salary-reports/download/fbd9e3ed-f64f-431f-94c1-c10783a519fc?locale=vi) likely contains the job-change %, layoff %, and city split.
