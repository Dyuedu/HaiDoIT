## (b) Leads
- TopCV financial statements: try masothue / the national business registry (dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn) or Vietstock company profile for CTCP TOPCV Việt Nam. Check the Pro/Premium billing period inside the app.
- en-japan: retry the IR library HTML (https://corp.en-japan.com/en/IR/library.html) and the quarterly 決算説明資料 for the "海外" segment and Vietnam lines.
- Findy: the second "2024" filing entry may be a corrected notice. Look for Findy press releases with "会員数" in 2026 to confirm the 267K/290K figures.
- Lightcast: search lightcast.io for "skill decay", "skills disruption" or "fastest-growing skills". Check whether Lightcast Open Skills or Global Postings covers Vietnam.
- Orbyt Jobs half-life engine: check its methodology. It is a direct conceptual competitor for the skill half-life feature.
- HRCV and X-Interview: check pricing pages and app-store installs.
- roadmap.sh: GitHub stars and SimilarWeb for traffic.
